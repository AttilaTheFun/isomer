# CoreTransferable: what Isomer does not have yet

Every declaration of Apple's CoreTransferable interface (iOS 27.0 SDK) that Isomer's CoreTransferable lacks, with Apple's signature: the list to choose what to implement next from. [CoreTransferable.md](../CoreTransferable.md) has the counts, the reasons and what is implemented differently. Generated by `tools/parity/parity.py` on 2026-10-23 with Isomer at `0a142780`.

Areas are ordered planned, then maybe, then the rest, larger first; within an area, types with the most missing first. "The type too" marks a type Isomer does not declare at all; elsewhere the type is there and these members are not. A version is the iOS release that introduced the declaration.

## Transfer representations (80)

Why: How a type of an app's own says what it can be transferred as (`transferRepresentation`, `DataRepresentation`, `FileRepresentation`, `ProxyRepresentation`, `CodableRepresentation` and their builder), and the standard types' own representations. The hosts' pickers and drops hand over files; reading one as an app's type through its representation is the next step, for drag and drop between views and to other apps.

Intent: planned.
